Blini

Summary

Flat yummy pancakes, better if its with condensed milk!

Ingredients

For pancakes

  • 300g plain flour
  • 2 teaspoons caster sugar
  • 6 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 pinch salt
  • 360ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 40g unsalted butter melted

For cooking

  • Butter for frying pan

Instructions

  1. Sift flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into a large bowl
  2. Gradually whisk in milk, egg and melted butter
  3. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add a little butter to the pan then drop heaped teaspoons of mix (10 ml) portions of blini mixture into the pan to form small rounds
  4. Cook until browned lightly underneath and bubbles appear on the surface. Turn over, and cook until blinis are cooked through
  5. Repeat with the remaining blini mixture. If the pan gets dirty from the burning butter, clean it out with a paper towel
